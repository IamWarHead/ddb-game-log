# Legendary Patrons

**Tier:** MAX, current or former.

Gamelog Config has a **Legendary Patrons** page. It lists the people who hold Gamelog MAX and chose to be on it, together with those who held MAX before.

{% include screenshot.html src="legendary-patrons.png" alt="Legendary Patrons page" %}

| What | Tier |
| --- | --- |
| Being on the list | MAX, now or in the past |
| Seeing the list | everyone |

## Put your name on it

Open **Gamelog Config → Legendary Patrons**. If you qualify, there is a field at the top: type the name you want to be shown and press **Put me on the list**.

- Nothing appears until you do this. Nothing is taken from your Patreon profile.
- Any name works: your own, your character's, your table's running joke.
- The list is public inside the module. Everyone who opens that page, in any world, sees it.

{: .note }
The name is chosen once. It stays with your Patreon account and cannot be edited afterwards, so pick one you will still like later.

## Hide and show

**Hide me** takes your name off the list. The name itself stays with your account, so **Show me again** puts the same one back. There is no way to swap it for a different name; ask on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/HSTtrphyFg) if a name has to be changed or removed for good.

## If your MAX ends

You stay on the list, in the second group, under "And those who were". Nothing to do, and nothing is lost if you come back later.

## Good to know

- The list is served by the Gamelog server and cached for a few minutes, so a change can take a moment to appear for other people.
- If the list cannot be loaded, the page says so; it has no effect on anything else in the module.
